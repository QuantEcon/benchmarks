## Reading

In float64, throughput collapses between 16k (2.05 GB) and 18k (2.59 GB), which straddles 2^31 bytes.
In float32 on the M1 Pro it holds to 31k (3.84 GB). On the x86 CPUs and the M6, float32 has also collapsed
by 40k (5.96 GiB). The threshold looks like a buffer size that depends on the dtype, in XLA:CPU's
fused reduction. Not yet traced: whether it is the reduction, the broadcast add or the -inf handling.
Untested: whether splitting the work into blocks under the threshold (as bellman_recompute does) avoids it.
